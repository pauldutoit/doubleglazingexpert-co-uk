## Frequently asked questions

**Will a window change need council approval in Edinburgh?**
The New Town and Old Town are a UNESCO World Heritage Site - any street-facing window change usually requires Listed Building Consent. Check your local planning portal before ordering - retrofitting a non-compliant frame is expensive.

**How much does a bay window cost in Edinburgh?**
Typically £1,140 to £3,040 in Edinburgh for the glazing, using the local 0.95x factor - plus £600 to £1,200 on top if a structural steel is needed, which a survey confirms.

**Will a new bay match a conservation area street?**
It can, but the profile and glazing bar pattern usually need council sign-off first. Get written pre-approval before the frames are made.
